# Privacy Policy — Luma: Light Puzzle

**Last updated: 12 November 2026**

Luma: Light Puzzle ("Luma") is an offline optical puzzle game for iOS and Android. The game does not collect personal data or send your gameplay progress to us. There is no Luma account, advertising, or developer-operated backend.

This policy also explains information you may choose to send when requesting support and the separate services provided by Apple and Google.

## Data stored on your device

Luma stores the following in its private local app storage:

- which levels you have completed;
- object positions, angles, and remaining instruments in unfinished rooms;
- your sound, haptics and hints preferences, and which introductions you have seen;
- whether "Reactive particles" is turned on, on devices that offer it;
- local recovery copies of these records, used to protect against an interrupted or unreadable save.

These records are used to continue your game and apply your preferences. Luma does not upload them to us, use them to identify you, or share them with advertisers.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or health data. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not use Game Center or Google Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, or achievements.

The Android game requests no internet permission, so it cannot open a network connection. Its only permission is vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`), used for haptic feedback.

## Motion sensors ("Reactive particles")

"Reactive particles" is off until you turn it on in Settings, and it is offered only on devices with suitable motion sensors (on iPhone and iPad, from iOS 26). When it is on, the game reads your device's motion — gravity and acceleration — while a room is on screen, to move the background particles. These readings are used on the device at that moment only: they are not stored and not sent anywhere. Turn the setting off and the game stops reading them.

## Storage, backups, and deletion

Data remains in the app's storage until it is updated, reset, or removed. A gameplay reset may retain local recovery copies; it is not a secure-erasure feature.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage from that device. Apple's device backups or transfer features may include app data, depending on your settings. On Android, when device backup is turned on, Android's backup to your Google Account and device transfer can include your completed levels, unfinished rooms, settings, introductions seen, the "Reactive particles" choice and their local recovery copies. These copies are made by the operating system, not by Luma, and are managed through your Apple or Google backup settings. We cannot see them. Deleting the app does not itself delete an existing device backup, and restoring a backup can bring your progress back. On Android, reinstalling the game can restore it too.

Luma does not currently offer its own cloud sync, export, or import feature. We have no server copy from which to restore your progress.

## Apple services

Apple operates the App Store, handles purchases, and may collect installation, transaction, diagnostic, and usage information under its own privacy terms and your settings. Apple may make relevant diagnostics or aggregated statistics available to developers. This is separate from data collected by the game, which has no analytics SDK.

If you use a TestFlight build, Apple automatically collects crash logs and beta usage information and shares them with us. Depending on how you were invited, beta information may include your name and email address. Feedback you submit, including screenshots and descriptions of an issue, is also shared with us. We use beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate issues and improve Luma. Apple manages retention within its TestFlight service under its own terms; contact us about information we have received through beta testing.

More information:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [App Analytics & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-analytics/)
- [TestFlight & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/)

## Google Play

Google operates Google Play and handles purchases under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details. Google may collect installation, transaction, diagnostic and usage information under its own privacy terms and your settings, and may make diagnostics or aggregated statistics available to developers. This is separate from the game, which has no analytics SDK.

Google's sales and earnings reports can include the buyer's country, sometimes region, city or postcode, and the model of the Android device used for the purchase. We use sales and payment reports for accounting, tax and payments.

If you join a test of Luma through Google Play, we hold the email address of the Google Account we invited, in Google Play Console or the Google Group used for the test, and any feedback you send us. We use tester addresses and feedback to run the test, investigate issues and improve Luma. Contact us about deleting information you have provided through testing.

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain support records. You can contact us about deleting correspondence you have provided.

Please do not send passwords, Apple Account or Google Account credentials, or payment details. Screenshots and recordings should show only information you intend to share.

If you open a GitHub issue, its content is public and is handled by GitHub under its own terms and privacy policy. These policy and support pages are also hosted on GitHub; visiting them is separate from playing the offline game.

## Children

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age. A parent or guardian can contact us with any privacy question or concern about information sent through support.

## Changes

If Luma's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. Earlier wording remains available in this file's repository history.
