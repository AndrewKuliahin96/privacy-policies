# Privacy Policy — Luma: Light Puzzle

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

Luma: Light Puzzle ("Luma") is an offline optical puzzle game. The game does not collect personal data or send your gameplay progress to us. There is no Luma account, advertising, or developer-operated backend.

This policy also explains information you may choose to send when requesting support and the separate services provided by Apple.

## Data stored on your device

Luma stores the following in its private local app storage:

- which levels you have completed;
- object positions, angles, and remaining instruments in unfinished rooms;
- your sound and haptics preferences;
- local recovery copies of progress or saved arrangements, used to protect against an interrupted or unreadable save.

These records are used to continue your game and apply your preferences. Luma does not upload them to us, use them to identify you, or share them with advertisers.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or health data. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not use Game Center sign-in, leaderboards, or achievements.

## Storage, backups, and deletion

Data remains in the app's storage until it is updated, reset, or removed. A gameplay reset may retain local recovery copies; it is not a secure-erasure feature.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage from that device. Apple's device backups or transfer features may include app data, depending on your settings. Those copies are managed by Apple and by your backup choices, rather than by a Luma sync service. Deleting the app does not itself delete an existing device backup.

Luma does not currently offer its own cloud sync, export, or import feature. We have no server copy from which to restore your progress.

## Apple services

Apple operates the App Store, handles purchases, and may collect installation, transaction, diagnostic, and usage information under its own privacy terms and your settings. Apple may make relevant diagnostics or aggregated statistics available to developers. This is separate from data collected by the game, which has no analytics SDK.

If you use a TestFlight build, Apple automatically collects crash logs and beta usage information and shares them with us. Depending on how you were invited, beta information may include your name and email address. Feedback you submit, including screenshots and descriptions of an issue, is also shared with us. We use beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate issues and improve Luma. Apple manages retention within its TestFlight service under its own terms; contact us about information we have received through beta testing.

More information:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [App Analytics & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-analytics/)
- [TestFlight & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/)

## Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain support records. You can contact us about deleting correspondence you have provided.

Please do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials, or payment details. Screenshots and recordings should show only information you intend to share.

If you open a GitHub issue, its content is public and is handled by GitHub under its own terms and privacy policy. These policy and support pages are also hosted on GitHub; visiting them is separate from playing the offline game.

## Children

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age. A parent or guardian can contact us with any privacy question or concern about information sent through support.

## Changes

If Luma's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. Earlier wording remains available in this file's repository history.
