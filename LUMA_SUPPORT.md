# Support — Luma: Light Puzzle

A quiet optical puzzle game about guiding light through mirrors and glass.

## How do I control the objects?

- **Rotate:** touch an object and drag around it. Move slowly for finer adjustment.
- **Move:** press and hold an object, then drag it to a new position. A placement that overlaps another object or a wall may be refused.
- **Place from the tray:** tap an instrument in the tray, then tap an empty place in the room. You can also drag an instrument from the tray into the room.
- **Reset one object:** double-tap it to return it to its starting angle and position. If its starting position is occupied, clear that space first.
- **Restart the room:** use the circular-arrow control beside the back control.

If you selected an instrument and want to manipulate something already in the room, tap the selected tray item again to deselect it.

## Why does a level not finish when the light reaches a receiver?

Each receiver needs enough of the light it responds to. A beam touching its edge may not provide enough light. Adjust the path until every receiver's indicator fills, and keep the arrangement steady briefly. In a room with several receivers, all of them must be filled together.

## Where is my progress saved?

Completed levels, unfinished room arrangements, and sound and haptics settings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. Replaying a completed level starts from its original arrangement.

There is no Luma account or developer-operated sync service. Support cannot retrieve a copy of your progress from a server.

See the [privacy policy](LUMA_PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for details.

## Can I move my progress to another device?

Luma does not currently include its own export, import, or cross-device sync feature. Apple's device transfer or backup-and-restore features may include app data, depending on your settings. Luma does not control or guarantee those processes.

Before deleting the app or replacing your device, check your Apple backup and transfer options. Reinstalling the app alone does not restore progress from a Luma server.

## What happens if I delete the app or reset progress?

Deleting the app removes its local app storage from that device. Copies included in an existing device backup are managed separately through Apple's backup settings.

Test builds may show an erase-progress control in Settings: press and hold the progress ring until it empties to restart your progress. This is a gameplay reset, not a secure-erasure tool; local recovery copies may remain. Restarting a single room does not erase your completed levels.

## How do I turn sound or haptics off?

Open Settings from the title screen. Use the speaker control for sound and the haptics control for vibration feedback. Haptic feedback also depends on device support and system settings.

## A level looks wrong, freezes, or cannot be completed

Please contact us with:

- your device model and iOS or iPadOS version;
- the app version and build number, if available;
- the level number and the steps that led to the problem;
- an optional screenshot or short screen recording showing the game.

For TestFlight builds, you can also use **Send Beta Feedback** in TestFlight. Please avoid including private information from notifications or other apps in screenshots or recordings. You do not need to delete the app to report a problem.
